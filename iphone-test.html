Save the Live Photo

  1. Tap Live Photo (zip) below and tap Download.
  2. Open Files > Downloads, tap the zip to unzip it, and open the folder.
  3. Tap Select, pick both the .JPG and the .MOV, then Share > Save 2 Items. Photos shows LIVE on it.

Set it as your lock screen

  1. Settings > Wallpaper > Add New Wallpaper > Photos.
  2. Pick the picture, and make sure the Live button (bottom left) is on.
  3. Tap Add, then Set as Wallpaper Pair. Press the lock screen to see it move.
