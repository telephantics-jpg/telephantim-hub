MYTHICAL WALLPAPERS

iPhone test · still and Live Photo

Option A: Live Photo from the zip (use Safari)

  1. Tap LIVE PHOTO (ZIP) under a scene. When Safari asks, tap Download.
  2. Open the Files app > Browse > Downloads (On My iPhone or iCloud Drive).
  3. Tap the zip once. Files makes a folder with the same name. Open that folder.
  4. Tap the three dots (top right) > Select, then tap both the photo (.HEIC) and the .MOV.
  5. Tap Share (bottom left) > Save 2 Items.
  6. Open Photos > Library. The newest picture should show LIVE in the top left. Press and hold it to see it move.

Save both files together in one go. One at a time gives a plain photo and a separate video. Do not tap the photo or video inside Files to open it first; just select them.

Option B: the two files on their own

  1. Under a scene, tap photo .HEIC > Download, then motion .MOV > Download.
  2. In Files > Downloads, Select both of them, then Share > Save 2 Items.

Set it as your lock screen

  1. Settings > Wallpaper > Add New Wallpaper > Photos.
  2. Pick the picture, and make sure the Live button (bottom left) is on.
  3. Tap Add, then Set as Wallpaper Pair. Press the lock screen to see it move.

Needs iOS 17 or later. A plain video can be saved to Photos (open the .MOV in Files > Share > Save Video), but iOS will not use a plain video as wallpaper; only a Live Photo moves on the lock screen.
