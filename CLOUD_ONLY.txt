Telephantix cloud (no PC required)
==================================

When your computer is OFF, use the live sites only:

  Hub (relics · bio · radio · Vox UI):
    https://telephantim.com/?v=92-vox-fix

  Camp brain + 2D/3D + DJ TTS (Render free):
    https://telephanti.com

How it wires together (nothing local):
  - telephantim.com  = static hub (GitHub Pages / Render static)
  - 2D/3D tabs       = iframe → https://telephanti.com/firmament/…?hub=1
  - DJ Vox           = POST → https://telephanti.com/api/firmament/dj/drop
  - Free edge-tts    = runs on telephanti.com Render box

You do NOT need:
  - 127.0.0.1:8765
  - 127.0.0.1:8767
  - Ollama on your PC
  - A second paid server

Notes:
  - Render free can "sleep" after idle. First open may take 30–60s.
    Vox client pings /api/health and retries once to wake it.
  - Music keeps playing in the browser tab until you close the browser
    (not your PC — the cloud serves files; audio still plays in the device browser).

Deploy:
  - Hub: push telephantim-hub master + gh-pages (auto static)
  - Camp: push telephanti-luna main → Render redeploys telephanti.com
