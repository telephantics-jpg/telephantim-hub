Mini Alpha

Mini Alpha is a free pocket companion with no subscription and no account. It chats, sketches beats, writes, draws and keeps a little home called the Den. Its mind runs right on your device, so it's private. The first time you load it, it downloads about 660 MB, then it works from your phone or PC. On iPhone, open it in Safari and tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

The zip is the whole app for tinkering. It needs a local web server to run (for example python -m http.server in the unzipped folder, then open localhost:8000/mini-alpha/).
